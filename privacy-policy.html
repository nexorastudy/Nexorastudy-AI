Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

NexoraStudy is an AI-powered educational application designed to help students solve doubts, access educational content, practice quizzes, and learn effectively.

1. No Account or Login Required

NexoraStudy does not require users to create an account or log in to use the application.

Users are not required to provide a name, email address, phone number, password, or other account information for registration.

2. Information We Process

When using NexoraStudy, users may submit questions, text, images, files, or other educational content to receive answers or assistance.

Information submitted by users may be transmitted to NexoraStudy's backend services and third-party services used to provide AI-powered responses or other application functionality.

Users should avoid submitting sensitive, confidential, or unnecessary personal information through the application.

3. AI Processing

NexoraStudy uses third-party AI services to process submitted questions and generate educational responses.

AI-generated responses may occasionally contain errors, inaccuracies, outdated information, or incomplete explanations. Users should independently verify important information using reliable sources.

4. Web Search

Some NexoraStudy features may use third-party web-search services to obtain current information or educational content.

When web search is used, the submitted query may be processed by the relevant third-party search service.

5. Permissions Used

  • Internet Access: Used to communicate with NexoraStudy's backend, AI services, web-search services, and other online services.
  • Microphone Access: Used for voice-based questions and learning features when enabled by the user.
  • File, Image, or Media Access: Used when the user chooses to select or upload educational files or images.

Permissions are used only for the relevant functionality of the application.

6. Data Usage

Information submitted to NexoraStudy is used to provide educational answers and application functionality.

NexoraStudy does not sell users' submitted questions or personal information to third parties.

Because NexoraStudy uses third-party service providers, information may be processed by those providers according to their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Third-Party Services

NexoraStudy may use third-party services for purposes such as:

  • AI-powered response generation
  • Web search
  • Cloud and backend hosting
  • Application functionality and infrastructure

These third-party providers may process information necessary to provide their services. Their own privacy policies and terms may also apply.

8. Data Retention

NexoraStudy does not provide user accounts or user profiles.

Questions and content submitted through the application may be temporarily processed by NexoraStudy's backend and third-party services to provide the requested functionality.

Technical logs or limited technical information may be retained by hosting or service providers according to their respective policies and operational requirements.

9. Data Security

NexoraStudy uses reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect information transmitted through the service.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee complete security.

Users should not submit passwords, financial information, highly sensitive personal information, or other confidential information through the application.

10. Children's Privacy

NexoraStudy is an educational application intended for students.

NexoraStudy does not require users to create accounts or knowingly request personal information from children for registration.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the service, please contact us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action where necessary.
